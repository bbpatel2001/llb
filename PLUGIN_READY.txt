🚀 QUICK INSTALLATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Step 1: Upload to WordPress
  • Go to Plugins → Add New
  • Click "Upload Plugin"
  • Select llb-knowledge-platform.zip
  • Click "Install Now"

Step 2: Activate
  • Click "Activate Plugin"

Step 3: Configure
  • Go to Settings → LLB Backend
  • Enter backend URL (e.g., http://localhost:6000)
  • Click "Test Connection"
  • Verify it shows "✅ Backend Online"
  • Click "Save Configuration"

Step 4: Use Shortcodes
  • Add shortcodes to pages/posts
  • Test backend status with [llb_backend_status]
