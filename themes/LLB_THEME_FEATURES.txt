✨ CUSTOM POST TYPES (5):

1. Question Papers (llb_question_paper)
   - Stores exam question papers from 10+ years
   - Meta fields: Exam Date, Total Marks, Duration, University
   - Taxonomies: Subject, Semester, Year
   - Archive: /question-papers/

2. Case Laws (llb_case_law)
   - Stores court judgments and case law
   - Meta fields: Citation, Judgment Year, Judges, Ratio Decidendi
   - Taxonomies: Subject, Year, Court
   - Archive: /case-laws/

3. Acts & Statutes (llb_statute)
   - Stores legislation and statutory provisions
   - Taxonomies: Subject
   - Archive: /acts-statutes/

4. Study Materials (llb_study_material)
   - Stores all study content (answers, flashcards, MCQs)
   - Taxonomies: Subject, Semester, Content Type
   - Archive: /study-materials/

5. Revision Notes (llb_revision_note)
   - Stores revision guides and notes
   - Taxonomies: Subject, Semester
   - Archive: /revision-notes/

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📊 CUSTOM TAXONOMIES (6):

1. llb_subject - Organize by legal subjects (Criminal Law, Contract Law, etc.)
2. llb_semester - Organize by semester (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6)
3. llb_year - Organize by exam year (2014-2024)
4. llb_content_type - Classify content (Short Answer, Long Answer, MCQ, etc.)
5. llb_court - Classify courts (Supreme Court, High Court, District Court)
6. Hierarchy - All taxonomies support hierarchical organization

🚀 HOW TO USE:

1. UPLOAD TO WORDPRESS:
   - Go to Admin → Appearance → Themes
   - Click "Upload Theme"
   - Select education-zone-llb folder (or zip it first)
   - Click Install and Activate

2. CREATE CONTENT:
   - Admin → Question Papers (create question papers)
   - Admin → Case Laws (create case law entries)
   - Admin → Acts & Statutes (create legislation)
   - Admin → Study Materials (create study content)
   - Admin → Revision Notes (create revision guides)

3. ORGANIZE WITH TAXONOMIES:
   - Assign subjects (Criminal Law, Contract Law, etc.)
   - Assign semesters (1-6)
   - Assign years (2014-2024)
   - Assign courts (if case law)
   - Assign content types (if study material)

4. VIEW ON FRONTEND:
   - /question-papers/ - Browse all question papers
   - /case-laws/ - Browse all case laws
   - /acts-statutes/ - Browse all legislation
   - /study-materials/ - Browse all study materials
   - /revision-notes/ - Browse all revision notes
   - /subject/criminal-law/ - Filter by subject
   - /semester/1/ - Filter by semester
   - /year/2024/ - Filter by year
